Hi! My name is Ying Ping. I grew up in Brooklyn, New York. I am Chinese American, and both my parents and my environment have shaped how I see the world. Living in Brooklyn exposed me to many different things, while watching Chinese dramas and reality shows I enjoyed the emotions, humor, and everyday life of the characters. My most memorable moment is staying up late at midnight playing catan with my siblings on the New Years.

I see myself as a ''still looking for what interests me'' artist who is still learning and exploring different paths I could take. I do not know exactly what kind of artist I want to become yet, but I am excited about the journey and the discoveries that will come with it.
